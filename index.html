Welcome to KCC

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served the community of Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental through Catholic education.

Guided by the call “Teach Ye All Nations” (Matthew 28:19), KCC seeks to form learners with knowledge, character, and competence.
